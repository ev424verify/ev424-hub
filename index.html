TL;DR
EV424 records a reproducible, non-custodial byte-sameness verification event (SHA-256 over raw bytes).
It does not judge truth/safety/meaning and does not host, mirror, or redistribute official documents. Not affiliated. Link-only.
Enter an ENTRY_ID or SHA-256 to view the deterministic FINAL RECEIPT and re-verify independently.
3-step
1) Input: EV424-ENTRY-000028 (example)
2) Output: FINAL RECEIPT (G1–G5 PASS/FAIL)
3) Re-verify: follow the receipt’s SHA-256 method (same bytes → same hash)
